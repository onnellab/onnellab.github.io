Landing page:
Papira assembles any finished TXT manuscript into a well-structured EPUB. It includes dedicated flows for fanfiction, serialized fiction, original novels, digital zines, and TRPG scenarios, while other TXT content can also be converted to EPUB.

## Key features

- Create an EPUB directly from TXT with Quick EPUB, without a cover or publishing metadata
- Build a book project with a cover, title, author, publisher and series details
- Detect chapters automatically, use # headings or keep a single chapter
- Build navigation and a table of contents, and review book details before export
- Use presets for fanfiction, serialized fiction, original novels, digital zines and TRPG scenarios, or convert other finished TXT content
- Preserve existing files by adding a number when an output name is already in use
- Create EPUB files and store book projects on your device

There is no account, sign-in, advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking SDK. TXT manuscripts, cover images, projects, previews, and generated EPUB files are processed locally and are not uploaded to an ONNELLAB server.

FAQ:
Q: What kinds of works can I prepare?
A: Any finished TXT content can be converted to EPUB. The dedicated presets simply make common creative workflows faster.
Q: Can I edit the manuscript in Papira?
A: Papira is for assembling a finished manuscript into EPUB. Edit the source TXT in your preferred writing tool first.
Q: Will exporting overwrite an existing file?
A: No. If the output name is already in use, Papira adds a number automatically and saves a separate file.
Q: Are manuscripts uploaded to a server?
A: No. TXT files, cover images, projects, previews, and generated EPUB files are handled on the device.
