Combina helps you organize videos without a complex editor. Join clips in your chosen order, trim to keep the part you want, or reduce a video’s file size.

## Key features

- Merge videos in the order you choose
- Select the start and end of a section to trim
- Check the estimated file size before compressing
- Save the result as a new MP4 while keeping the originals
- Continue with the saved video to trim or compress it

Free supports up to three videos per merge, one trim interval, and the Low compression preset. A one-time Pro purchase removes the merge-count limit and adds more compression presets and multiple trim intervals saved as separate files.

Core video processing happens on your device. Combina does not upload your videos to a Combina server.

FAQ:
Q: Can I choose the order of the videos?
A: Yes. Arrange the selected videos in the order you want before merging them.

Q: What happens to the original videos?
A: Combina creates a new MP4 result and does not modify or delete the originals.

Q: Are videos uploaded to a server?
A: Core processing happens on your device, and videos are not uploaded to a Combina server.

Q: What does Pro add?
A: Free supports up to three videos per merge, one trim interval and Low compression. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the merge-count limit, adds Balanced and Sharp compression, and saves multiple trim intervals as separate files.
