프로모션 텍스트:
Prepare stickers and acrylic keyrings. Review background transparency for eligible images, set finished sizes, then export supported PNG or PDF files.

설명:
Meriq helps you prepare print files for stickers, acrylic keyrings and photo cards. Choose recommended dimensions or enter your own finished size, then place your artwork on the front or back in one project.

## Review background transparency before applying it

For eligible opaque artwork in clear acrylic keyring and die-cut sticker projects, Meriq suggests a transparent-background result on your device. Review the result, apply it, refine the selection or keep the original image.

## Prepare cut lines, white underprint and exports together

- Choose product-specific recommended sizes or set custom finished dimensions
- Place front and back artwork and edit production layers
- Prepare vector cut lines and a white underprint layer beneath your artwork
- Prepare and edit supported finishing layers; finishing features require Meriq Pro
- Check physical size and image resolution with a basic Production Check
- Export transparent PNG files and PDFs with the production content combined
- Transfer editable projects in standard ZIP project packages

## Check your print provider's requirements

PDF exports combine the production content; they do not contain independently editable production layers. Before sending files for printing, confirm your print provider's file format and size requirements.

## Your artwork stays on your device

Projects and imported images are stored on your device. Preview and export also happen locally. No account or login is needed. There are no ads or cloud sync. You choose the files to import, and export, sharing and project transfer begin only when you start them. Image and project content is not written to diagnostic logs.

FAQ:
Q: Does Meriq upload my projects or images to a server?
A: No. Projects and imported images stay on your device, and preview and export happen locally. Your artwork is not uploaded to ONNELLAB servers.

Q: Which file formats can I export?
A: You can export transparent PNG files and PDFs with the production content combined. PDFs do not contain independently editable production layers. Use a standard ZIP project package to transfer an editable project.

Q: Do finishing features require Pro?
A: Yes. Preparing and editing supported finishing layers requires Meriq Pro.

Q: Do purchases or restores upload my project content?
A: No. The App Store or Google Play processes purchases and restores. Project content is not uploaded as part of that process.

Q: Does exporting or sharing happen automatically?
A: No. Exporting, sharing and ZIP project transfer start only when you choose them.
