# Lunary major observances, schema 1

This versioned, read-only data supplement contains 29 selected annual observances:
14 Korean national/family observances and 15 international public-interest days.
The supported date-rule window is 2026–2035. It is not a complete global calendar.
National/family coverage outside South Korea and cultural/religious catalogues are
not provided by this version. International entries are optional display labels,
not declarations that every country legally recognizes or closes for the date.

The JSON never makes a date a public holiday, substitute holiday or paid-leave
day. This supplement must remain separate from public-holiday classification;
consumers must not infer that classification from event titles. Date rules are
civil dates, not UTC instants.

Each entry records a stable identifier, country scope, category, date rule,
independently authored short names in nine app locales, official source URL and
factual verification date. The version/verification date describes this reviewed
pack, not a government's latest announcement. Future dates project current rules
and may be corrected, moved or removed through a later reviewed version.

## Source and rights boundary

Korean date rules are independently structured from legislation. Korean Copyright
Act Article 7 excludes statutes, orders and rules from copyright protection:
https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0007&lsiSeq=283335&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR
The source and amendments are recorded here:
https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsRvsDocListP.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsId=002065&lsRvsGubun=all

International dates and short factual designations are checked against official
UN, WHO and UNESCO sources, with a specific URL retained in each record. The
selection, structure and brief localized labels were independently written for
Lunary. No campaign paragraphs, article text, images, logos or photographs are
included. UN website content is not generally open-licensed; this pack is not
UN-licensed data, an endorsed product or blanket permission to republish source
websites. Do not expand it by copying protected descriptions or artwork.
https://www.un.org/en/about-us/terms-of-use
https://www.un.org/en/observances/list-days-weeks

## Delivery and maintenance

Compatible Lunary versions bundle a reviewed offline copy. An independently versioned copy
can be published at the existing ONNELLAB static site and fetched separately from
public holidays. Publication alone does not prove that an installed app refreshes
it: compatible app code must validate the schema, bounds and response, and keep
the last good copy when unavailable. No instant official-announcement SLA is
provided. A successful fetch timestamp is not the source's change timestamp.

Review changes before publication. Replace the complete pack to support removed
or corrected entries; preserve stable identifiers where the concept is unchanged.
Check official sources monthly, comprehensively review annually, and review new
verified announcements when discovered. Automated monitoring is a separate
operational responsibility, not provided merely by this static file.
