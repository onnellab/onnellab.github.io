Meriq Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Meriq app provided by ONNELLAB.

Last updated: 2026-08-23

Privacy Policy

Meriq values your privacy. This app operates as described below.

1. Accounts and direct identifiers

Meriq does not require registration or sign-in with a name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data accessed or stored on the device

  • Project files, imported resources, and metadata you explicitly select
  • Project preferences, purchase entitlement status, and local app settings
  • Project, preview, export, and image content processing occurs on the device.
  • Selected files are not uploaded to an ONNELLAB server.
  • Only files you explicitly choose via the system file picker are used.

3. Server transmission

App content (projects, images, usage data) is not sent to ONNELLAB servers. Diagnostic logs do not include file contents.

4. Payment information

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Neither Meriq nor ONNELLAB stores or collects card or bank-account details. Store-supplied product, transaction, and entitlement status is used only to verify and restore entitlements.

5. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Meriq does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. Data is not sold or shared outside the purposes described above.

If content is handed to another app or service through the operating-system share sheet, that service's privacy and usage policy applies.

6. Retention and deletion

Local app-managed data can be removed through in-app delete actions or by uninstalling the app. Source and output files selected by the user remain under user control.

7. Security and children's privacy

ONNELLAB applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to necessary transmitted data. Meriq is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

If app functionality or legal/store requirements change, this document will be updated and its last-updated date will change.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: onnellab.app@gmail.com
